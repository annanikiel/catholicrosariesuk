Mission

Prayer in the middle of everyday life

Most of us don’t live in a monastery. Our days are full of work, family, traffic and noise. Being a contemplative in action means finding God there, in the middle of it all, rather than waiting for a quiet moment that never comes.

A rosary is a small, practical way to do that. It sits in a pocket or bag, and a single decade fits into a commute or the minutes before a meeting. With the beads in your hand, you can find that inner silence wherever you are.

Beads

Beads made to be held

People have counted prayers on beads, seeds and knotted cord for centuries. The word rosary comes from the Latin rosarium, a rose garden. Today I work with materials chosen to last through years of daily use.

Gemstone

Agate

A banded form of quartz that builds up layer by layer over thousands of years, so no two beads are alike. It’s hard and scratch-resistant, one of the most durable stones for a rosary.

Gemstone

Ammonite

Polished fossil of a sea creature that lived millions of years ago, with the patterns of its shell still visible. It’s softer than agate, so it’s best kept away from keys and coins.

Glass

Glass pearl

A glass core with a pearl coating. Heavier and smoother than plastic, with a soft lustre. Keep away from perfume and cleaning products to protect the finish.

Wood

Wood

One of the oldest materials for prayer beads. Light, warm and quiet in the hand, and it mellows with use. Keep it dry. More woods are coming soon.

Bead sizes

6mm: slimmer and lighter. Sits neatly in a pocket.

8mm: fuller in the hand and easier to count by touch.

Devotions

Praying the Rosary

The Rosary walks through the life of Christ alongside Mary. Each decade has a mystery to reflect on as you pray. Pray all five decades, or just one when that’s all the day allows.

How to pray it

  1. On the crucifix, make the Sign of the Cross and say the Apostles’ Creed.
  2. On the first bead, an Our Father. On the next three, a Hail Mary each, then a Glory Be.
  3. Announce the first mystery, then pray an Our Father on the single bead.
  4. Pray ten Hail Marys on the decade, then a Glory Be and the Fatima Prayer.
  5. Repeat for the other four decades, and finish with the Hail, Holy Queen at the centre medal.

The prayers

The Sign of the Cross

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

The Apostles’ Creed

I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth, and in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord, who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died and was buried; he descended into hell; on the third day he rose again from the dead; he ascended into heaven, and is seated at the right hand of God the Father almighty; from there he will come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy catholic Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Amen.

Our Father

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come, thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

Hail Mary

Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee; blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

Glory Be

Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit; as it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

The Fatima Prayer

O my Jesus, forgive us our sins, save us from the fires of hell; lead all souls to heaven, especially those in most need of thy mercy.

Hail, Holy Queen

Hail, holy Queen, Mother of mercy, hail, our life, our sweetness and our hope. To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve; to thee do we send up our sighs, mourning and weeping in this vale of tears. Turn then, most gracious advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us, and after this our exile show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus. O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary. V. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God. R. That we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ.
